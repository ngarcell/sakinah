## Long Description Draft

`Sakinah is a private marriage ritual app for Muslim couples who want to stay emotionally close in a calmer, more intentional way.

Begin with one thoughtful prompt a day, then keep your shared reflections, letters, goals, memories, and check-ins in one place designed for the two of you.

Inside Sakinah:

- Daily prompts that make meaningful conversation easier
- Guided weekly reflection to help you notice what needs care
- A shared journal for gratitude, honesty, and everyday moments
- Private letters for words you want to send with intention
- Shared goals and wishlists that keep your life aligned
- Du’a and faith-aware content that support spiritual connection
- A private shared space you invite your spouse into through iCloud

Sakinah is built for couples who want something quieter than social media, more personal than generic couple games, and more natural than relationship homework.

Use it in a few minutes a day, or return whenever you need a gentle way back to each other.`
