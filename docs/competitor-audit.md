# Sakinah Competitor Audit

Audit date: May 3, 2026

Method:
- Official App Store listings and official product/support websites only.
- Prices below are what the US App Store pages exposed on May 3, 2026. Subscription pricing can vary by storefront, experiment, or promo.
- When onboarding or paywall timing is not explicitly stated in official copy, it is marked as an inference from official screenshots, listing language, or support documentation.

## Summary

Sakinah should not compete as a generic couples game, therapy substitute, or AI relationship coach. The strongest position is:

- Premium Muslim marriage ritual app
- Calm, private, faith-rooted, and spouse-specific
- Daily first-value loop with one saved answer before the paywall
- Hard paywall after the first answer
- Annual plan featured with a 7-day free trial
- Shared private space as a true product promise, not just copy

## 1. Paired

Official sources:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paired-couples-relationship/id1469609343
- Website: https://www.paired.com/
- Premium support article: https://support.paired.com/en/articles/164633-what-s-paired-premium
- Free-trial campaign page: https://couplish.paired.com/

Observed position:
- Mass-market relationship habit app for busy couples
- Daily questions, games, quizzes, expert content, and relationship insights

Monetization:
- Freemium
- Support article says free users get a new conversation every day
- Premium unlocks 1,000+ conversations, games, journeys, insights, and expert material
- Support article says one paying partner unlocks premium for the other paired partner
- US App Store listing showed monthly `14.99 USD` and multiple annual SKUs including `39.99 USD` and `74.99 USD`
- Campaign page explicitly advertises `7 days free`

Onboarding and paywall timing:
- Inference: daily-question-first product
- Inference: free layer is intentionally meaningful before premium pressure
- Inference: paywall likely appears after initial setup or deeper feature access rather than immediately on cold open

Strengths:
- Very clear daily habit framing
- Expert-backed credibility without feeling clinical
- Strong breadth across quizzes, journeys, and games
- Two-seat unlock is a major conversion advantage

Friction and gaps:
- Tone is broader and more playful than premium
- Positioning is relationship-wide, not marriage-specific
- Free layer makes conversion softer but also lowers perceived exclusivity
- Not privacy-first in the way Sakinah can be

Implication for Sakinah:
- Keep the daily habit clarity
- Keep the sense of emotional reward after both partners answer
- Avoid the playful everything-app sprawl
- Differentiate with marriage specificity, calmer tone, faith-rooted content, and cleaner premium intent

## 2. Lasting

Official sources:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lasting-marriage-couples/id1225049619
- Terms: https://getlasting.com/terms
- Therapist-code landing page: https://getlasting.com/redeem-therapist-code

Observed position:
- Serious guided relationship counseling app
- Therapy-coded, research-heavy, and structured around sessions

Monetization:
- Freemium plus premium subscription
- App Store copy says free access includes Foundations, conversation starters, reminders, and single sessions
- App Store copy says Lasting Premium unlocks the full app for two users
- App Store copy says monthly and yearly subscriptions include a free 7-day trial
- US App Store listing showed monthly `11.99 USD` and `14.99 USD`, plus annual-style premium SKUs around `79.99 USD`
- Therapist landing page advertises a separate `30-day free trial` path for organization codes

Onboarding and paywall timing:
- Inference: assessment-led, needs-based onboarding
- Inference: free Foundations reduce first-session friction before the main premium ask
- Observed from copy: the product is structured around unlocking a large premium session library

Strengths:
- High trust and seriousness
- Clear premium value ladder
- Strong two-user access story
- Good topic depth for communication, conflict, trust, sex, money, and family

Friction and gaps:
- Therapy-coded framing can feel heavy or remedial
- Less romantic and less ritual-based
- Faith and religious language are not core differentiators

Implication for Sakinah:
- Borrow the trust, maturity, and assessment-style seriousness
- Avoid feeling like counseling homework
- Use marriage ritual and spiritual intimacy as the emotional center

## 3. Agapé

Official sources:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/agap%C3%A9-feel-close-when-apart/id1507907556

Observed position:
- Relationship wellness app for couples, family, and friends
- Daily question engine with personalized categories and memory features

Monetization:
- Freemium
- App Store copy explicitly says the app is free to download and free to use
- App Store copy says users can answer thousands of questions for free
- Premium unlocks premium categories
- US App Store listing showed monthly SKUs at `9.99 USD` and `14.99 USD`, annual SKUs at `19.99 USD`, `49.99 USD`, `59.99 USD`, and `99.99 USD`, plus a `199.99 USD` lifetime SKU

Onboarding and paywall timing:
- Inference: daily-question-first and connection-first
- App Store version history says users can answer questions before they connect with people they care about most on the app
- Inference: premium pressure is later and category-based, not immediate

Strengths:
- Very clear one-question-a-day simplicity
- Warm emotional tone
- Strong personalization language
- Broad relationship graph beyond romantic partners

Friction and gaps:
- Less premium and less focused
- Not marriage-specific
- Multi-relationship positioning dilutes spouse-specific value
- Free abundance lowers exclusivity and urgency

Implication for Sakinah:
- Keep the simplicity of one meaningful question
- Stay focused on the spouse relationship only
- Make the product feel more deliberate, private, and adult than Agapé

## 4. Couply

Official sources:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/couply-couples-relationship/id1484241314
- Main site: https://www.couply.io/
- Premium FAQ: https://www.couply.io/premium-faqs

Observed position:
- Broad couples super-app
- Quizzes, daily questions, AI coach, articles, date ideas, courses, long-distance mode, memories, milestones, and community

Monetization:
- Freemium
- App Store copy explicitly says it can be used for free
- Premium FAQ says free access is generous, while premium unlocks all quiz questions, full weekly content, shared notes, all courses, all conversation packs, and premium articles
- US App Store listing showed premium SKUs at `9.49 USD`, `14.99 USD`, `69.99 USD`, and `70.99 USD`
- Official promo page for a partner program advertised a `1 month free trial`, but that appears to be campaign-specific rather than the default consumer funnel

Onboarding and paywall timing:
- Inference: broad value-stacking onboarding
- Inference: paywall is likely delayed because the free layer is part of the pitch
- Inference: AI and community make the app feel bigger, but also noisier and less premium

Strengths:
- Large perceived value stack
- Personality and quiz depth
- Clear long-distance and AI differentiation
- Content breadth is hard to ignore

Friction and gaps:
- Noisy positioning
- AI and community can cheapen trust for intimate use cases
- Less calm, less private, and less premium
- Product sprawl increases cognitive load

Implication for Sakinah:
- Do not copy the super-app breadth
- Win on restraint, calmness, privacy, and product discipline
- Keep the core loop tight and emotionally credible

## 5. Evergreen

Official sources:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/evergreen-relationship-growth/id1573360122/

Observed position:
- Relationship growth app with daily questions, quizzes, games, lessons, check-ins, and expert tips

Monetization:
- Free with in-app purchases
- US App Store listing showed premium SKUs including `9.99 USD`, `12.99 USD`, `29.99 USD`, `49.99 USD`, and `69.99 USD`
- Inference from official listing and reviews: meaningful free usage exists before purchase

Onboarding and paywall timing:
- Inference: soft onboarding and habit-first framing
- Inference: daily question loop likely appears before full premium pressure

Strengths:
- Calm design reputation
- Daily growth framing is accessible
- Lightweight enough for casual adoption

Friction and gaps:
- Positioning is generic
- Little religious, cultural, or marriage-specific depth
- Monetization story is less explicit and less premium than leaders like Paired or Lasting

Implication for Sakinah:
- Keep the calmness
- Add sharper differentiation and premium clarity

## Best Market Position For Sakinah

Sakinah should sit between Lasting and Agapé:

- More serious and trustworthy than Agapé
- More intimate and ritual-based than Lasting
- More focused and premium than Couply
- More marriage-specific than Paired or Evergreen

That means:

- Hard paywall after a real saved first answer
- No freemium
- Annual featured with a 7-day free trial
- Calm premium copy
- Muslim marriage framing as a real differentiator, not surface decoration
- Shared private space as a working feature, not a promise
